Pricing Questions

How does PuppetPC keep my data private?

PuppetPC creates encrypted tunnels between your devices. Your services are never exposed to the public internet. No inbound ports need to be opened.

What happens when I need more than 2 devices?

Most people start with a laptop and a server. But once you add a NAS, a Raspberry Pi, a second workstation, or a cloud VM, you'll want Pro. Upgrading is instant and gives you unlimited devices for $2/month.

How does PuppetPC compare to alternatives?

PuppetPC is purpose-built for private tunneling with full device management. Unlike raw WireGuard, there's no manual key management. Unlike Cloudflare Tunnel, your services stay completely private.
